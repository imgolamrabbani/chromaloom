Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

ChromaLoom operates on a strict zero-telemetry, minimal-permission Manifest V3 architecture.

1. Zero Telemetry & No <all_urls>

ChromaLoom does not collect browsing history, page URLs, DOM contents, cookies, usage analytics, or crash reports. Active-tab inspection runs strictly on demand when you click the extension.

2. Outbound Network Requests

ChromaLoom makes zero outbound network requests unless you explicitly activate a Polar.sh Pro license key, in which case it communicates only with https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys/* to activate, validate (every 72 hours when online), or deactivate your key.
